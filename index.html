    On the job

    What I Build at Work

    As an IT Manager at a manufacturer, I don't just keep the lights on. I build the tools the business runs on, connected to the ERP, CRM and Microsoft 365 systems people already use.

    Internal request hub

    One place for 10 kinds of company requests: returns, material issues, IT support, invoice disputes, maintenance and engineering changes. Smart multi-step forms flow straight into HubSpot.

    Visitor sign-in system

    Tablet kiosk check-in for the head office and factory. Hosts get a Microsoft Teams and email alert, reception sees who is on site live, and staff sign in with Microsoft 365.

    Shipping label printing

    Scan a sales order and print the shipping labels on a Zebra printer, using live data from the ERP. Built as a Windows app for the shop floor and as a web app.

    QuickBooks sync

    Near real-time sync from a cloud database into QuickBooks Desktop through the QuickBooks Web Connector, with a dashboard to watch every transaction go through.

    Invoice generator

    Replaced a retired Microsoft Publisher template. Upload the order PDFs and it fills in the invoice for you, shows a live preview, saves it and exports a PDF.

    Product configurator

    A guided 5-step configurator for car wash equipment, with live pricing, a build summary and quote requests ready for the sales team in HubSpot.

    Car wash site analysis

    Enter any address and get a GO, CAUTION or NO-GO verdict: nearby competitors and their reviews from Google Maps, a traffic score and a 5-year financial projection.

    Car wash operations platform

    In progress: equipment, work orders and downtime for car wash operators, with an offline-ready phone app, photos and per-company data security.

    Client websites

    Fast, search-friendly websites for small businesses, including a site and printable capability statement for a veteran-owned government contractor.

    About

    About Me

    I'm an IT professional and AI engineer who likes technology that is secure, simple and actually used.

    Day to day, I run technology for businesses in manufacturing, construction and automation: networks and firewalls, Microsoft 365 (including SharePoint and Power Platform), CRM platforms like HubSpot, Salesforce and NetSuite, and the cloud. I lead cross-functional teams and automate the busywork so people can focus on what matters.

    I also design and build AI apps and agents on Microsoft Azure with Microsoft Foundry, from retrieval-augmented generation (RAG) that puts company knowledge to work, to multi-agent workflows for everyday processes. Security, data governance and responsible AI come first, not last.

    I'm CISSP certified, an ITIL Expert, Cisco CCNA and CCNP, and hold Microsoft certifications across Microsoft 365, Azure security and AI, plus HubSpot and Salesforce. As a Microsoft Certified Trainer, I also love helping teams get more from the tools they already have.
