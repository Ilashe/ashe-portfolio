Verba

Every meeting, captured.

Record or upload a meeting and Verba writes the transcript, a short summary, the decisions and the action items, with owners and due dates. The speech recognition and the AI both run on your own computer, so recordings never leave it and there is nothing to pay per minute.

  • ~100 languages
  • 100% on your computer
  • $0 per minute
  • 55 automated tests

The problem

AI note-takers are useful, but most of them send every word of your meetings to someone else's cloud and charge by the minute.

The idea

Run the whole thing locally: open speech recognition and an open AI model, on the same computer that holds the recording.

The result

Notes you can act on, from meetings that stay private. It even works offline once the models are downloaded.

What makes it different

Answers you can check

Ask a question like “What did we decide about the launch?” and Verba answers from the meeting itself. Every answer points to the moments it came from, and one click plays that moment, so you never have to take the AI's word for it.

  • Cited moments: answers may only cite lines the AI was actually given
  • Prompt-injection safe: someone saying “ignore your instructions” in a meeting can't steer the notes
  • Chapters snap to the exact line where each topic starts
  • Long meetings are summarised in parts, then merged

Features

From recording to action items

Capture and transcribe

  • Record your microphone, optionally mixed with a browser tab for online meetings
  • Upload MP3, M4A, WAV, MP4, MOV and more, including Teams and Zoom recordings
  • About 100 languages with Whisper, plus translation into English
  • Three accuracy levels; the transcript appears line by line as it listens

Notes you can act on

  • Summary, key points, decisions and open questions
  • Action items with owners and due dates that you can tick off
  • Search every meeting at once; the player follows the transcript
  • Export to Word, Markdown, text and subtitles, or copy notes for email
