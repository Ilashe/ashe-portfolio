CRM & help desk SaaS · Small businesses in the USA and Canada

KOINORIX

Connecting people. Growing business.

One place for a small team to win deals and keep customers happy: leads, a drag-and-drop sales pipeline, forecasts against targets, and a help desk with response timers and a customer portal. Simple enough for a five-person shop, with the security of a much bigger product.

  • Sales + support in one app
  • Every workspace isolated in the database
  • 507 automated tests
  • Light & dark, phone-ready

The problem

Small businesses juggle a CRM, a separate help desk and a pile of spreadsheets, and pay per seat for each. Sales never sees the support side of a customer.

The idea

Put leads, deals, contacts and support tickets in one app that is quick to learn, with the same customer record behind all of them.

The result

A team sees the whole customer: the deal they are negotiating, the ticket that is overdue, and the files and history behind both.

Sales

See every deal and what it's worth

Drag deals across stages, or move them with the keyboard. Each stage shows its value and win probability, and the forecast compares won, committed and weighted revenue with each person's monthly target.

  • Leads that convert into a contact, company and deal in one step
  • Forecast by month or quarter, with targets per person
  • Automations that create tasks and assign work when things change
  • Reports you build yourself and pin to the dashboard

Support

Never lose a customer issue again

Tickets with priorities, owners and response and solve timers. Overdue work gets its own view, and the database stamps every deadline so forms, bulk edits and automations always agree.

  • SLA timers per priority: on track, due soon, overdue, met
  • Customer portal: customers send requests and follow them on a private link
  • Public replies kept separate from internal notes, so nothing leaks
  • Custom fields such as product or serial number, with filters and exports
