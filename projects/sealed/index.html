Sealed

Signed, sealed and verifiable.

Electronic signatures for businesses, in the same space as DocuSign. Every finished document is digitally sealed, timestamped by an independent authority, and comes with a certificate anyone can verify without uploading the file.

  • 11 languages
  • PAdES digital signature
  • 62 automated tests
  • WCAG AA accessible

The problem

A signature is only as good as your ability to prove it later: who signed, when, and that nothing changed since.

The idea

Make the proof part of the document itself, so it stands on its own, in any PDF reader, years from now.

The result

One sealed PDF: every signature, a signing certificate, a digital signature and a trusted timestamp. Any change shows.

What makes it different

Sealed, not just signed

When the last person finishes, Sealed produces the final PDF once and signs it. The signing certificate lists every signer, their identity check, time zone, device and a tamper-evident trail where each step is chained to the one before.

  • PDF digital signature (PAdES) with an RFC 3161 trusted timestamp
  • Bitcoin timestamp proof (OpenTimestamps) that nobody can backdate
  • Verify page: the file is fingerprinted in the browser and never uploaded
  • Validated with an independent PDF signature checker: intact, whole-file coverage

Features

Built for senders and signers

For the people signing

  • No account: a private link, a clear consent step, sign on any phone
  • 11 languages, including Arabic right to left; dates in their own time zone
  • Type or draw a signature; dropdowns, choices, ticks and file uploads
  • Identity checks: an access code or a code sent to their phone
  • Decline with a reason, or pass it to the right colleague

For the business sending

  • Upload a PDF, Word file or photo; drag fields into place
  • Signers, approvers and copies, all at once or in order; sign in person
  • Templates and bulk send from a spreadsheet, with automatic reminders
  • Your logo and colour on signing pages, emails and certificates
  • API and signed webhooks to connect other systems
