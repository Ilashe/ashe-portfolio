Spot the Phish

Phishing training your team will actually finish. A 5-minute daily challenge, instant lessons on every red flag, and the proof your auditors ask for.

  • 5 min a day
  • 40+ realistic emails
  • 187 automated tests
  • WCAG AA accessible

The problem

Most breaches start with one click. Yearly 45-minute training videos are skipped, forgotten, or both.

The idea

Practise like a game, five minutes a day, on emails that look exactly like the real thing.

The result

Staff build a habit. Admins see readiness rise and export proof for auditors and cyber-insurance.

Signature mechanic

Tap the red flag

Reporting a phish is good. Knowing why is better. Before reporting, people flag the exact part that gave it away: the sender, the subject, a link, an attachment or the message itself.

  • +25 points for every real red flag, −10 for a wrong one, so guessing doesn't pay
  • Scored on the server, so nobody can cheat the leaderboard
  • Instant feedback shows what was spotted, what was missed, and why

Features

Built for both sides

For staff

  • Daily challenge: 10 new emails a day, one scored try, fair team leaderboards
  • Adaptive practice that learns each person's weak spots
  • Phone view that hides the sender, just like real mail apps
  • XP, levels and streaks, earned only by correct answers
  • No passwords: sign in with an email link, Microsoft or Google

For admins

  • Readiness score (accuracy + weekly participation) with next best actions
  • Assigned training with due dates, pass marks, reminders and PDF certificates
  • Reports: board-ready PDF and CSV exports, plus a full activity log
  • Microsoft 365 staff sync: new starters in, leavers out, automatically
  • Ctrl+K quick search and a notifications bell
